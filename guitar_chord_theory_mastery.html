Scenario 2

Eliminating Giant Hand Leaps via Voice Leading

Jumping between low open chords across the neck creates harsh pauses. Smooth Voice Leading keeps common notes anchored while shifting adjacent notes by just 1 or 2 frets in high register inversions (Frets 12-17).

Scenario 3

Unlocking the Full 24-Fret Octave Loop

Every pattern on Frets 0-11 repeats identically on Frets 12-23, one octave higher! The 24th fret marks the second complete octave loop. See how the C Major chord formula aligns identically across both octaves.

Major Triad (1 - 3 - 5)

Bright, grounded, stable. Formed by Root + 4 semitones (Major 3rd) + 3 semitones (Perfect 5th).

Minor Triad (1 - ♭3 - 5)

Melancholic, sad, introspective. Lowering the 3rd by 1 semitone changes major to minor.

Suspended 4th (1 - 4 - 5)

Replaces the 3rd with the 4th interval. Creates tension longing to resolve back down to the 3rd.

Add 9 Chord (1 - 3 - 5 - 9)

Adds the 2nd octave note (9th) to a basic major triad without adding a 7th interval.
